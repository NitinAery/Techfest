Morning ritual printed in scarlet.

SCRATCH FERMENTATION

Laminated dough. 48 hours.

Our country sourdough ferments through two full revolutions of the sun. We fold cultured Normandy butter twenty-seven times into stoneground flour before baking in a high-thermal hearth at 5:00 AM.

Direct Trade

Sourced from family estates in Huila, Colombia & Yirgacheffe, paying 180% above Fairtrade baseline.

Slow Ferment

Wild levain culture nurtured for 7 years, delivering open crumb structure and deep caramelized crust.

Small Batches

Roasted in 12kg increments on our custom Loring smart roaster to safeguard delicate floral aromatics.

THE COFFEE RITUAL

Precision in every pour.

We dial in our espresso profiles every morning before sunrise, measuring water salinity, extraction yield, and grind micron distribution. The result is a cup with the clarity of print: no bitter astringency, just pure fruit, floral, and caramelized sugars.
